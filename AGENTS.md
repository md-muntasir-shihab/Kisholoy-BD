# KISHOLOY Project Instructions

1. **Strictly follow the 22-Phase Playbook.**
2. Execute ONE phase at a time. Do not jump ahead.

6. Bangla and English are first-class languages. Ensure proper font and fallback support.
7. Implement robust error handling, retries, and audit logs for all critical operations.
8. At the end of every phase, provide the exact phase completion report.

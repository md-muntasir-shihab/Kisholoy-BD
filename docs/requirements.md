# Requirements Matrix

## Core Modules (MVP)
- **Storefront:** Homepage, Product Listing, Product Detail, Cart, Checkout, Order Tracking.
- **Admin Control Center:** Dashboard, Content Management, Operations.
- **Catalog Management:** Products, Categories, Variants, Inventory.
- **Order Management:** Orders, Shipments, Returns, Refunds.
- **Payments:** COD, Online Payment Adapter (SSLCOMMERZ).
- **Logistics:** Courier Service Adapter (Steadfast/Pathao), Barcode/QR generation.
- **Customers & Auth:** RBAC, Customer Profiles.
- **Automation & Notifications:** Webhooks, SMS/Email/In-app, Retries, Queue.
- **Finance & Reporting:** Expenses, Reconciliation, Reports, Documents (PDF).
- **Infrastructure:** Backups, Audit Logs, Settings.

## Admin-Controllable Content
- Logo, Favicon, Brand Info (Name, Description, Headline, Tagline, Motto).
- Hero Content, Banners, Homepage Sections.
- Categories, Featured Products, Navigation, Footer, Policies, Notification Templates.
